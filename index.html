Sarang Gosavi

PhD Student | Mechanical Engineering

University of Colorado Boulder

I am a PhD student in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Colorado Boulder, working in plasmonics and its applications in catalysis, with a strong focus on experimental research.

My work involves developing and studying material systems and catalytic processes to understand how light-matter interactions can influence chemical transformations.

My broader interests include emerging areas in quantum technologies, nanomaterials, and advanced energy-related systems. I am particularly interested in research at the intersection of fundamental science, experimental engineering, and technologies with potential real-world impact.
