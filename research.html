Plasmonic Photocatalysis

University of Colorado Boulder

Designed and developed a custom reactor system for plasmonic catalysis, successfully demonstrating the conversion of ammonia into hydrogen.

Ongoing research aims to extend this platform toward the complete destruction of per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) through light-driven catalytic processes.

August 2023 – December 2023

Crystals for Hydrogen Storage

Performed ab initio density functional theory (DFT) calculations using Quantum ESPRESSO to investigate the ground-state energies of crystalline materials for hydrogen storage applications, with the goal of identifying promising materials for efficient hydrogen storage.
